Build (Part 1)Resource group, VM, NSG rules, Apache. Checkpoint: HTTP 200 on the Azure IP.
Capture (Part 2)Make the OS cloud-neutral, freeze the root filesystem, qemu-img to a compressed qcow2 on the scratch disk.
Transfer (Part 3)Download, verify checksum, remove the link, convert to raw, tar with the oldgnu format, upload to the bucket.
Launch (Parts 4 and 5)Image from the bucket, VM from the image, firewall rule, HTTP 200 on the GCP IP, hostname apacheserver1 inside.
Clean up (Part 7)destroy-lab1.sh: delete the resource group, the GCP VM, image, bucket and firewall rule.

Dashed boxes are network scope. Bold boxes are the migrated workload. Data path: Azure OS disk to qcow2 (compressed) to laptop to raw to tar.gz to Cloud Storage to Compute Engine image to VM boot disk. Nothing in GCP is created until Part 3, which is why the lab can be torn down and rebuilt in 25 minutes.
