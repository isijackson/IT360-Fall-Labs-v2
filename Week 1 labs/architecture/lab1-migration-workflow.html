Four lanes, one procedure. The source cloud builds and captures; the laptop (or Cloud Shell) carries and converts; Google Cloud imports and boots. Where the time goes: the frozen raw copy and the download, both proportional to disk size, not to data. Lab 2 is where the same servers get configured; this lab only moves them.

Three failure modes found on the way, now gates in the procedure: unprepared OS (cloud-init polls the old metadata service; emergency shell), frozen but unrepaired copy (initramfs fsck: Resize inode not valid), stale whole-disk cache (copy claims 2 GB; e2fsck clears the root; kernel panic at /sbin/init).
